Obsidian Suite: the finished amps

These ten heads have their NAM captures wired in. Drag a knob up or down (or scroll on it, double-click to reset), use the buttons to switch channels, and click the power lamp to shut an amp down and watch the lights and tubes die out. Under each amp: which capture is loading right now, and what every control does.

Captures come from your Tone3000 downloads; file names are shown without .nam. Accuracy scores are the error figures stored in the captures where the creator included them (lower is closer to the real amp).
